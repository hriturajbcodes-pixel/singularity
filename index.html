FAQ

Common questions

What is Singularity?

Singularity builds TradingView tools for traders who work with higher-timeframe structure and correlated markets. The tools give context and alerts. They do not give financial advice and they do not predict price.

What do I get when I join?

You get access to the tools in your plan. Claim them in a few steps: join, connect your TradingView username, and add the indicator to a chart. Your membership is linked to that username.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 5-day free trial. You can cancel anytime during the trial and you will not be charged.

Do the tools work on all markets?

They are built for any market that has correlated instruments and clear higher-timeframe structure — FX, indices, crypto, futures and large-cap equities all work well.

How often are the tools updated?

We ship continuous improvements. Members receive updates automatically. Major new models are announced in advance and rolled into the existing suite.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ultimate+ and Full Suite plans include a 14-day money-back guarantee if the tools are not a fit.
